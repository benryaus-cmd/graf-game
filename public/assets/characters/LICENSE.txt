Original official Drive downloads returned quota-exceeded HTML, including
both glTF and FBX endpoints. The mirror GLBs are complete animated re-exports
of the selected pack models, with their authored materials and skeletons.
All 17 original animation clips are preserved; no invented clips or geometry.
No external texture/buffer URLs or Google Drive requests are used at runtime.
