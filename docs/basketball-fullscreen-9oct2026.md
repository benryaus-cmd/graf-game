# Basketball screen controls and hoop correction

The basketball panel previously covered the hoop and limited shooting to a small pad. Basketball now accepts an upward flick across the game viewport, with score, Leave and five shooting spots kept at the edges. Power/aim feedback appears only during the gesture. Interactive HUD controls stay above the gesture surface; menu entry, focus loss and pointer cancellation abandon a pending shot. The same short-edge reference keeps flick strength consistent across start locations and rotated layouts, and long upward swipes clamp to the existing launch limits.

The rim opening is 30% larger. Its radius and tube scale together, and the centre shifts slightly toward the court to preserve the existing backboard gap. The net and scoring effects derive from the shared rim dimensions. Backboard paint dimensions, surface ordering and artwork addresses remain unchanged.

Ball collisions sweep each bounded physics step against the rim and backboard, including glancing rim contacts and board edges/corners. A shot scores only on an unobstructed downward crossing inside the opening. Gravity, bounce restitution, five shooting marks, three-second lifetime and pooled presentation remain lightweight; no new physics dependency, lights or shadows are added.
